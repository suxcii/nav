SFBU: PLACES ON CAMPUS (Main Building)

Q: What is in the library? What books does the library have?
A: The Library is in the Main Building. It is a quiet place for studying, reading, and completing assignments. The documents do not list which books the library has.

Q: What food is in the cafeteria? What does the cafeteria serve?
A: The documents do not list a cafeteria menu. The campus map shows a Cafeteria on the 1st floor. For restaurants near campus, ask "where can I eat near campus".

Q: What is in the Main Building?
A: The Main Building has the LRC (Learning Resource Center), the Library, teaching faculty offices, a computer lab, and a game room. Many classes take place here, especially in the classrooms upstairs. The front desk is also in the Main Building.

Q: What is the LRC?
A: The LRC (Learning Resource Center) is in the Main Building. It is where most student socializing happens, and some clubs meet there, such as the Chess Club and the Crochet Club.
