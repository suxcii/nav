OVERVIEW
The Career Center is dedicated to helping all SFBU students prepare for their future careers. Services include 1:1 career coaching, employer connection events, internship support, and career education.

Contact: career-services@sfbu.edu | (510) 803-7428 ext. 4
Key Contact: Kenrick Ali, Director of Career Services
Location: Student Success Hub (SSH)

---

SERVICES FOR STUDENTS

1. One-on-One Career Coaching
Meet with Kenrick Ali for personalized guidance on:
- Resume and cover letter writing
- LinkedIn profile optimization
- Job and internship search strategy
- Interview preparation and mock interviews
- CPT (Curricular Practical Training) and OPT (Optional Practical Training) arrangements

2. Handshake Platform
Handshake is the #1 way SFBU students find jobs and internships.
- Connect with employers
- Explore career options
- Apply for jobs and internships
- Get discovered by recruiters
- RSVP for campus events and career fairs
- Schedule 1:1 advising appointments
Register using your SFBU email credentials when the school year begins.

3. Career Studio (Walk-In Support)
The Career Studio at the Student Success Hub offers walk-in support for:
- Resume and cover letter help
- CPT navigation
- Handshake setup
- LinkedIn optimization
- Mock interview sessions

4. Career Workshops and Fairs
Throughout the semester, the Career Center hosts:
- Career fairs connecting students with employers
- Resume workshops
- LinkedIn profile workshops
- Interview skills sessions
- Industry networking events

5. Peer Mentorship Program — "Under My Wing"
Incoming students are paired 1:1 with a current student who has similar academic interests, professional pursuits, or life experiences.
- Mentors and mentees meet at least once a month
- Topics: career options, work-life balance, social success, campus life
- You will be paired with a Peer Mentor at the start of the academic year

6. Micro-Internships (Parker Dewey)
Short-term, paid, typically remote professional projects available to all students regardless of major.
- Available any time of year
- Companies range from Fortune 100 to startups
- Facilitated through the Parker Dewey platform
- Great for building skills and exploring career paths without a long-term commitment

7. Virtual Internships
SFBU has partnered with Virtual Internships to provide guaranteed, free internship placements on a first-come, first-served basis.
- Complete from your own laptop, anywhere
- Domestic students can apply for Spring 2026 placements
- International students must have completed one year of full-time study before applying

CPT (CURRICULAR PRACTICAL TRAINING) — KEY FACTS FOR INTERNATIONAL STUDENTS

CPT allows F-1 international students to work in a paid internship or cooperative education position that is directly related to their program of study.

Eligibility:
- F-1 students must complete two full-time semesters at SFBU before applying
- U.S. citizens and permanent residents are eligible immediately

How to Apply:
- Submit via student portal: My Requests → Academic Requests → CPT Application
- Deadline: Week 11 of the intended working semester — apply early!

Important Rules:
- No more than 6 units of CPT coursework may count toward graduation
- Taking more than 12 months of full-time CPT affects OPT eligibility after graduation
- Part-time CPT does not affect OPT eligibility
- CPT reports are graded Satisfactory (S) / No Pass (NP)
- Two NP grades = CPT terminated and failing grade issued

Social Security Number:
If you don't have an SSN, your CPT advisor will provide a DSO letter to support your application at a local Social Security Administration office.

Contact for CPT/OPT questions: Kenrick Ali or Jessie Dhamrait (Designated School Official)

---

OPT (OPTIONAL PRACTICAL TRAINING)

OPT allows students to work in their field of study after graduation. Contact the Career Center or International Student Services (iss@sfbu.edu) for details on how to apply.
