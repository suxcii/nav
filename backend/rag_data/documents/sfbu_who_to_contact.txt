SFBU: WHO TO CONTACT (quick answers for common problems)

Q: I lost my AC Transit bus pass (EasyPass) or my bus card. What do I do?
A: The documents do not describe a replacement process. The EasyPass is picked up at the front desk of the Main Building, so ask there first. You can also contact Michael Leinhos (Student Life), who handles EasyPass questions.

Q: I lost my student ID card. What do I do?
A: The ID card is picked up at the front desk, so ask at the front desk of the Main Building. For other questions, contact Michael Leinhos (Student Life).

Q: I have a question and I don't know who to ask. Where do I go?
A: Start at the front desk of the Main Building. Front desk staff can also help you schedule a meeting with a staff member.

Q: Who do I contact about a bus pass, student clubs or Student Life?
A: Michael Leinhos (Student Life), or the Student Life office.

Q: Who do I contact about my tuition bill?
A: Email studentaccounts@sfbu.edu, or ask at the front desk to schedule a time with the Bursar.

Q: Who do I contact about housing?
A: Email housing@sfbu.edu.

Q: Who do I contact about mental health or counseling?
A: Email wellness@sfbu.edu, or call (510) 803-7328 ext. 185. The Wellness office is in the Student Success Hub. TimelyCare is free and available 24/7 online.

Q: Who do I contact about jobs, internships, CPT or OPT?
A: Career Center: career-services@sfbu.edu or (510) 803-7428 ext. 4. For international students, International Student Services: iss@sfbu.edu.

Q: Who do I contact if I missed orientation?
A: Email orientation@sfbu.edu.

Q: Who do I contact about health insurance billing?
A: Email health-insurance@sfbu.edu.

Q: Who do I contact to make a complaint or grievance?
A: Submit it in writing to the Compliance Team at compliance@sfbu.edu.

Q: I'm upset, stressed, lonely, or having a problem with a friend or roommate. Who can I talk to?
A: Free counseling is available. TimelyCare gives you 12 free counseling sessions per year, 24/7 and online (sign up at app.timelycare.com with your SFBU email). You can also book in-person counseling by emailing wellness@sfbu.edu.

Q: I feel unsafe, someone is stalking or harassing me, or someone is threatening me. What should I do?
A: If you are in immediate danger, call 911. SFBU prohibits harassment, discrimination and intimidation, and students are encouraged to report any concern or incident. You can reach out to the Dean's Office (Dean of Students, Dr. Kafele Khalfani), which handles student concerns and Title IX and Equity. The Dean of Students or the Title IX office can issue a No Contact Order. A security guard is on site before standard business hours and through evening classes. For emotional support, email wellness@sfbu.edu, use TimelyCare (free, 24/7), or call non-emergency crisis support at 1-800-309-2131 (24/7).
