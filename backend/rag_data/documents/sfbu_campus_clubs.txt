OVERVIEW
SFBU has a wide variety of student clubs covering academics, arts, sports, community service, and social interests. Students can also start their own clubs. Getting involved in clubs is one of the best ways to meet people and adjust to university life.

CURRENT CLUBS AND MEETING TIMES

Art and Expression Club (currently on hold)
Every Tuesday | 1 PM - 2 PM | Student Success Hub
A creative space to explore artistic passions, share work, and connect through various art forms.

Basketball Club
Every Saturday | 3:30 PM - 5:30 PM | Warm Springs Park
Open to all skill levels. Great for staying active and meeting peers.

Business Club
Last Tuesday of each month | 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM | Learning Resource Center
Workshops, networking, and real-world projects for students interested in leadership and entrepreneurship.

Chess Club
Every Tuesday | 4 PM - 5 PM | Learning Resource Center
Friendly competition and strategy for players of all skill levels.

Crochet Club
Every Thursday | 1 PM - 2 PM | Learning Resource Center
Crafting and crochet sessions. SFBU provides basic supplies. Research shows crafting boosts well-being!

Dance Club
Every Thursday | 12 PM - 1 PM | Student Lounge
Open to all skill levels. Learn, perform, and connect through dance and movement.

Entrepreneurship Club
2nd and 4th Wednesdays of the month | 1 PM - 2 PM | Room 102
Hands-on innovation, networking, and startup resources for aspiring entrepreneurs.

Hiking Club
Meets periodically for local Bay Area hikes.
Explore trails around Fremont and Silicon Valley with fellow students.

International Students Club
Community and social support for international students navigating life at SFBU and in the U.S.

IEEE Club (Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers)
For students in tech, engineering, and computer science fields.

Toastmasters Club
Public speaking and leadership development in a supportive environment.

Badminton Club
Founded by a student. Open to all levels. Check campus boards for meeting times.

Research and Publication Club
For students interested in academic research and writing.

Fashion Club
Meets periodically. For students passionate about fashion and style.

Soccer Club
Recreational soccer for all skill levels.

HOW TO JOIN
- Visit club tables at orientation
- Check campus notice boards for meeting times and updates
- Contact Michael Leinhos (Student Life) for information: email via SFBU directory
- Join the SGA (Student Government Association) WhatsApp group for announcements

HOW TO START A CLUB
Any student can start a new club. Contact Michael Leinhos or the Student Life office. Previous students have successfully started clubs like Badminton and others from scratch.
