OVERVIEW
SFBU prioritizes wellness holistically, paying equal attention to students' physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual health. Wellness is considered one of the six pillars of the student experience at SFBU.

Office hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM
Location: Student Success Hub (SSH)
Contact: wellness@sfbu.edu | (510) 803-7328 ext. 185

---

SFBU WELLNESS SERVICES

1. Counseling Services
Licensed therapists provide individual and group counseling to support mental health, manage stress, and address personal challenges. Appointments are confidential.
- To schedule: email wellness@sfbu.edu
- Key counselors: Beth Tsang (Licensed School Therapist), Dr. Kell Fujimoto (Licensed School Therapist)
- Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM
- Location: Student Success Hub

2. TimelyCare (FREE Virtual Healthcare — 24/7)
All SFBU students have free, 24/7 access to TimelyCare — a virtual health and wellbeing platform.
- Services include: medical advice, emotional support, wellness coaching, self-care tools
- Students get 12 FREE counseling sessions per year
- No insurance required
- Sign up with your SFBU email at: app.timelycare.com
- Available 365 days a year

3. Wellness Workshops
Regular workshops and seminars on topics including:
- Stress management
- Mindfulness and meditation
- Sleep hygiene
- Substance abuse prevention
- Physical wellness (yoga, Zumba, pickleball, sound healing)
Weekly Wellness Workshops are held every Tuesday in the Student Success Hub.

4. Crisis Intervention
Immediate support and resources are available for students experiencing emergencies or crises.
- For emergencies: call 911
- For non-emergency crisis support: 1-800-309-2131 (24/7)

5. Let'sTALK Program
A peer and staff support program that trains select students and staff to listen and support others. Designated supporters can be identified by a pink bracelet or Let'sTALK pin. Not a replacement for therapy — a first step for open conversations.

6. Disability and Accessibility Services
Contact: Margaux John, Coordinator for Disability and Accessibility Services
For students who need accommodations for physical or learning disabilities.
More info: sfbu.edu/accessibility-and-disability

HEALTH INSURANCE AT SFBU

All SFBU students are required to have health insurance. The university provides a group health insurance plan for both domestic and international students.

Students who already have coverage (through a parent's employer plan or Medi-Cal) may request a waiver. Waivers must be submitted by the specified deadline.

For billing questions related to health insurance: health-insurance@sfbu.edu

STUDENT EMERGENCY FUND
SFBU has a Student Emergency Fund for students facing unexpected financial hardship. Contact the Dean of Student Services or visit sfbu.edu/student-life-support/student-emergency-fund for more information.

FOOD PANTRY AND CLOTHING CLOSET
Located in the Student Success Hub. Students can access free food supplies, basic groceries, and professional clothing for job interviews or career events. Available to any SFBU student in need.
