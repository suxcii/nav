OVERVIEW
Living in university housing offers the benefit of being close to campus and an opportunity to live and learn alongside other SFBU students. Housing is limited and availability varies by student type.

HOUSING ELIGIBILITY
- Startup Scholars: guaranteed fall and spring semester housing with other Startup Scholars or undergraduate students.
- Other undergraduate and graduate students: housing in university-owned residences is limited and offered through a lottery system.
- Residents must have submitted an enrollment deposit to be eligible.

STANDARD HOUSING DETAILS
Housing units are two-bedroom with double occupancy — meaning four students share a two-bedroom unit. A small number of three-bedroom units accommodate six students.

Each bedroom includes:
- Two beds
- Two desks
- Two chairs

Shared unit amenities:
- Washer and dryer in the unit
- Kitchen table and chairs
- High-speed internet connection
- Refrigerator and microwave
- Couch and coffee table
- Microwave
- Cleaning supplies (mop, broom, dust pan)
- Pedestal fan
- Twin bed with mattress cover
- Study desk with lamp

WHAT TO BRING (not provided)
- Bedding and pillow
- Shower/bathroom mat(s)
- Shower caddy
- Trash can bags
- Surge protector
- Laundry bag/basket and detergent
- Clothes hangers
- Towels (kitchen, shower, bathroom)
- Drying rack
- Plastic storage bags
- Silverware
- Dish soap and sponge
- Aluminum foil and plastic wrap
- Additional lighting (if desired)
- Room decor (NOTE: no tape or push pins allowed on walls)

MOVE-IN INFORMATION
- Standard move-in day: Wednesday before orientation week (e.g., August 13 for Fall 2025)
- Startup Scholars have a separate, earlier move-in date
- Students should check their email and the SFBU Residential Life page for updates

HELPFUL TIPS FOR HOUSING (from student experience)
- Fremont housing can be expensive — plan your budget early
- Start looking for off-campus housing before the semester begins
- Many students share apartments with roommates to reduce costs
- When choosing housing, consider distance to campus and available transportation options
- On-campus housing is close and convenient, but spaces are limited — apply early
