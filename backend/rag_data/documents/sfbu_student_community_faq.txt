DOCUMENT: SFBU Student Community FAQ — Common Questions from New Students

NOTE: This document captures the types of questions and answers that circulate in SFBU student WhatsApp groups, peer chats, and informal community channels. It is based on information from official SFBU sources and student experience.

---

Q: I just got accepted. What's the first thing I should do?
A: Check your SFBU email immediately — this is how the university communicates everything important, including orientation details, housing lottery invitations, Canvas access, and your enrollment counselor's contact info. Don't ignore it or check it only occasionally.

Q: When should I arrive on campus?
A: Move-in for on-campus housing is typically the Wednesday before orientation week (August 13 for Fall 2025). Orientation itself is August 14 or 15 — you only need to attend one day. Classes start August 18. Try to arrive at least a few days before orientation so you have time to get settled.

Q: Which airport should I fly into?
A: SJC (San Jose Mineta) is closest — only 10 miles from campus. An Uber from SJC is about 15 minutes and costs $15-20. OAK and SFO are cheaper to fly into but farther away. From OAK, BART + Uber takes about 1 hour and costs $15-25. From SFO, it takes 1.5+ hours.

Q: Is there a free bus pass?
A: Yes! Full-time students get the AC Transit SFBU EasyPass — a free annual bus pass. Pick it up from the front desk of the Main Building within your first week. For BART, you need to buy a separate Clipper card at any BART station.

Q: How do I get my student ID?
A: Your photo is taken at orientation. The physical ID card is usually ready within 1-2 weeks and can be picked up at the front desk.

Q: I missed the orientation. Is that okay?
A: Try not to miss it. Orientation is where you get your accounts set up, meet key staff, learn about resources, and get your photo taken for your ID. If you miss it, contact orientation@sfbu.edu.

Q: What is Canvas?
A: Canvas is the online platform where all your classes live — syllabus, assignments, grades, professor announcements, and Alma (the AI tutor). Set up notifications immediately so you don't miss deadlines.

Q: How long do I have to add or drop classes?
A: Only about one week after classes start (e.g., August 18–23 for Fall 2025). After that window closes, you can only withdraw — which leaves a "W" on your transcript. Act fast if you want to change your schedule.

Q: I'm struggling in my class. What should I do?
A: Don't wait until you're failing. Go to your professor's office hours first. For math support, see Evelyn Binoya. For English and writing, see Leslie Ji. Both are in the Student Success Hub. You can also use Alma inside Canvas for Gen-Ed courses.

Q: Is there free mental health support?
A: Yes — two options. TimelyCare gives you 12 free counseling sessions per year, 24/7 virtual access. Sign up at app.timelycare.com with your SFBU email. You can also book in-person counseling with Beth Tsang or Dr. Kell Fujimoto by emailing wellness@sfbu.edu.

Q: I'm an international student. How do I work legally in the US?
A: After completing two full-time semesters at SFBU, you can apply for CPT (Curricular Practical Training), which lets you do paid internships related to your major. Apply through your student portal. Talk to Kenrick Ali (Career Services) or Jessie Dhamrait (your Designated School Official) for guidance.

Q: Is on-campus housing guaranteed?
A: Only for Startup Scholars. For all other students, housing is limited and awarded through a lottery. Apply as early as possible. Fremont is expensive — if you don't get on-campus housing, start looking for shared apartments early.

Q: What's the cheapest way to get groceries?
A: Safeway (0.3 miles) and 99 Ranch Market (0.5 miles) are the closest stores. For budget shopping, Grocery Outlet (4 miles) is great. For Asian groceries, Osaka Marketplace (0.3 miles) is right next door. You can also use delivery apps like Weee! or Instacart.

Q: How do I make friends at SFBU?
A: The LRC (Learning Resource Center) in the Main Building is where most student socializing happens — just show up and people will talk to you. Join at least one club in your first month. SFBU is small, so you'll see the same faces regularly, which makes forming friendships easier than at large universities.

Q: What clubs are there?
A: Lots! Badminton, Basketball, Chess, Crochet, Dance, Entrepreneurship, Hiking, IEEE, International Students Club, Toastmasters, Business Club, Art Club, Fashion Club, Soccer, and more. You can also start your own club — contact Michael Leinhos in Student Life.

Q: I'm having issues with my tuition bill. Who do I contact?
A: Email studentaccounts@sfbu.edu or speak with Edie Millet (the Bursar) by visiting the front desk and scheduling a time through Lesley Jimenez (Front Desk Receptionist).

Q: Who do I talk to about visa and immigration questions?
A: Jessie Dhamrait, the Designated School Official. Schedule a meeting through the front desk.

Q: What's the Student Success Hub (SSH)?
A: The SSH is SFBU's second main building and is entirely dedicated to student support. It houses counseling, career services, academic advising, tutoring, disability services, the food pantry, and more. If you ever need help with anything, the SSH is your first stop.

Q: Is there free food on campus?
A: Yes — the Food Pantry in the SSH provides free food supplies and basic groceries for any student who needs it. There's also a Clothing Closet with professional attire for interviews. No judgment, no forms — just go.

Q: How do I stay updated on events and announcements?
A: Follow SFBU on Instagram (@sanfranciscobayuniversity), join the Student WhatsApp Channel, check Canvas announcements, and keep an eye on notice boards in the LRC and SSH.
