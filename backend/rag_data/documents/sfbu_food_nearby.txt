SFBU: WHERE TO EAT (restaurants near campus, from the NSO handbook)

Q: Where can I eat near campus? What restaurants are nearby?
A: The handbook lists nearby coffee shops and restaurants. These are restaurants around campus, not the campus cafeteria. For all dining options, see Pacific Commons Fremont. The campus map shows a Cafeteria on the 1st floor, but the documents do not say what it serves.

Q: What Asian restaurants are near campus?
A: Boiling Point Hotpot (Chinese), Burma Bay Cafe (Burmese), Karikku Cafe (Indian), Kazuma Sushi (Japanese), Mom's Tofu (Korean), Pho Ha Noi (Vietnamese), Ramen Arashi (Japanese), R-HaaN Thai Restaurant (Thai).

Q: What bakeries and healthy food are near campus?
A: Bakeries: Forest & Flour, Paris Baguette, Pineapple King, Tous Les Jours, 85 Degrees Bakery. Healthy options: Panera Bread, Healthy Tiffin, RAW Superfood Cafe, Vitalty Bowl.

Q: What Mediterranean, Mexican and pizza places are near campus?
A: Mediterranean: Dish N Dash, Kabob Fusion, Mikonos Grill, The Kebab Shop. Mexican: El Rodeo, La Pinata Restaurant, Los Dos Gallos Taqueria 2, Taqueria Las Vegas, Spartan Tacos. Pizza: Curry Pizza House, Round Table Pizza, Straw Hat Pizza, Blaze Pizza, Pieology Pizzeria.

Q: Where can I get coffee or dessert near campus?
A: Coffee shops: Devout Coffee, Hops and Beans Cafe, Qamaria Yemeni Coffee Co., Tamper Room Cafe & Gallery. Desserts: Mandro Teahouse, Cauldron Ice Cream, Tong Sui Desserts & Drinks, Meet Fresh, ICICLES, Bruster's Real Ice Cream.

Q: Where are the nearest grocery stores and pharmacy?
A: Within 1 mile: Safeway Grocery (0.3 miles), Osaka Marketplace (0.3 miles), CVS Pharmacy (0.5 miles), 99 Ranch (0.5 miles).
