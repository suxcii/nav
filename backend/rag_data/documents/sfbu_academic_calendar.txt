KEY REMINDERS FOR NEW STUDENTS

- The Add/Drop window is only about ONE WEEK after classes start. If you want to change your schedule, act immediately.
- Withdrawing from a class (after the drop period but before the last withdrawal date) will result in a "W" on your transcript but won't affect your GPA.
- Dropping after the last withdrawal date may result in a failing grade.
- Always check your SFBU email and Canvas for updates to these dates.
- The academic calendar is also available at: sfbu.edu/important-dates
